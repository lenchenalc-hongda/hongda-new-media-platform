# 04 AI 承接与转人工规则

> SPEC_LOCK_VERSION = GLH-SPEC-V1.0
> FROZEN = YES

## AI role

AI = overseas lead front desk + salesperson copilot.

## First inbound loop

On customer inbound:

1. persist original event/message first;
2. identify language;
3. detect business intent;
4. extract known structured fields;
5. ask only the 1-3 most important missing questions at a time;
6. update completeness and priority;
7. decide whether AI may continue or must hand off.

AI failure must never cause the original customer message to disappear.

## Qualification fields

Core:

- product;
- image;
- material;
- quantity;
- country;
- requirement type.

Additional as relevant:

- dimensions;
- artwork;
- printing area;
- current process;
- pain points;
- test requirements;
- sample availability;
- purchase timing.

Machine inquiries additionally ask:

- product dimensions;
- target production capacity;
- current machines/process;
- desired automation level;
- whether a full machine + film/process solution is required.

## Initial scoring model (100 points)

- clear product image: +15
- material known: +15
- quantity known: +15
- company/site/credible business identity: +10
- clear film/processing/machine requirement: +15
- test requirement or sample intention: +10
- target industry fit: +10
- repeat/long-term order or clear buying timeline: +10

Grades:

- A = 80-100
- B = 60-79
- C = 30-59
- D = 0-29

Score is advisory priority, not deletion authority.

## Immediate human handoff triggers

Any one can trigger `READY_FOR_HUMAN / HANDOFF_PENDING`:

- asks for quotation/price decision;
- asks for sample/testing;
- machine inquiry;
- clear large-volume/important commercial opportunity;
- sends formal RFQ/spec/drawing;
- asks for video meeting;
- asks for final technical confirmation;
- payment/delivery/discount terms;
- distributor/agency discussion;
- complaint/dissatisfaction;
- AI fails to resolve after two useful turns;
- low-confidence/high-risk classification;
- administrator-configured important-account rule.

## Forbidden autonomous claims

AI must not state or imply:

- confirmed final price or discount;
- confirmed delivery date;
- confirmed payment terms;
- "definitely can do" without approved technical basis;
- adhesion/test will definitely pass;
- confirmed final machine output/capacity;
- compensation;
- contract/agency commitment.

## Human takeover

When a salesperson clicks Take Over:

- conversation mode becomes `HUMAN_ACTIVE`;
- AI automatic customer-facing sends stop;
- AI still drafts summaries, missing info, next action and reply suggestions;
- salesperson may use/edit/ignore drafts.

Automatic follow-up after human takeover is out of V1 unless explicitly approved later.

Prompt versions and AI action outcomes must be auditable.
