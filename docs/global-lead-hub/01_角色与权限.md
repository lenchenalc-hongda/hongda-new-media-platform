# 01 角色与权限

> SPEC_LOCK_VERSION = GLH-SPEC-V1.0
> FROZEN = YES

## Admin

Can:

- view all GLH records in the organization;
- manage users/roles/assignment rules/channel config references;
- reassign leads;
- see audit and analytics.

Cannot:

- bypass Production safety gates;
- silently erase message/audit history;
- turn AI proposals into binding price/technical facts without human confirmation.

## Manager

Can:

- view team-wide leads and conversations;
- assign/reassign;
- correct grades/stages;
- see workload/lead-quality analytics;
- resolve exceptions.

## Sales

Can:

- see assigned leads and explicitly shared/collaborative records allowed by policy;
- take over eligible leads;
- chat as human;
- update permitted business fields/stages;
- create follow-up tasks;
- request quotation/sample progression.

Must not gain implicit access to another salesperson's restricted lead solely because they are authenticated.

## AI

AI is server-side automation, not a user account with broad business permissions.

AI may:

- classify;
- extract facts from customer messages;
- ask qualification questions;
- score priority;
- draft replies;
- create handoff/task suggestions;
- send only within explicitly permitted `AI_ACTIVE` conditions.

AI may not autonomously commit:

- final prices / discounts;
- payment terms;
- delivery promises;
- final technical feasibility;
- adhesion/test guarantees;
- machine capacity guarantees;
- compensation;
- contracts;
- distributor/agency commitments.

## Authorization requirements

- RLS on every exposed `glh_` table.
- Authorization data must not rely on user-editable `user_metadata`.
- Business user foreign keys use `profiles.id`.
- Never expose service-role keys to browser code.
- Assignment, grade, stage, AI-send, handoff, channel-setting and role changes are audited.
