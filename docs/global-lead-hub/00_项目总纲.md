# 00_项目总纲

## Product definition

Hongda Global Lead Hub is the company-owned lead intake and qualification layer between Meta traffic and the overseas sales team.

It must solve:
- too many inbound leads for one salesperson;
- missed/unanswered leads;
- repeated basic qualification questions;
- customer information trapped in individual phones/accounts;
- inability to distinguish high-value leads quickly;
- weak follow-up discipline;
- inability to connect ad source to downstream business quality.

## Architecture layers

1. Channel intake — Facebook / Instagram / WhatsApp.
2. AI intake — first response, language detection, requirement classification, missing-info collection.
3. Qualification — profile, completeness, A/B/C/D priority.
4. Human handoff — assignment, takeover, transfer, conversation.
5. Sales progression — quotation, sample, negotiation, won/lost.
6. Follow-up — tasks, overdue, dormant.
7. Management review — salesperson workload, lead quality, funnel, ad-source quality.

## Roles

V1 roles:
- Admin
- Manager
- Sales
- AI automation (not a human profile)

## Channel phases

P0:
- WhatsApp Business Platform / Cloud API
- Facebook/Instagram click-to-WhatsApp attribution when Meta provides referral/ad metadata

P1:
- Facebook Messenger
- Instagram DM

P2:
- Facebook Lead Forms
- LinkedIn integrations

## Core product rules

- One company-owned lead master record per real customer/opportunity context.
- `owner_profile_id` means current salesperson responsibility, not ownership of the customer asset.
- Preserve original messages; AI summaries are derived data only.
- Every inbound webhook is idempotent.
- After human takeover, AI becomes Copilot and may not continue autonomous customer-facing replies.
- No hard-delete of lead/message/audit history from normal business UI.
- Production execution is always a separate explicit human gate.

## Non-goals for V1

Do not build:
- ERP / finance / payment accounting;
- automatic pricing or discount approval;
- contract generation/approval;
- delivery-date guarantees;
- automatic technical guarantees;
- LinkedIn scraping/bulk automation;
- large BI warehouse;
- legacy `/leads` migration;
- autonomous Production deployment.
